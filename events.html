Our Story and Mission

Riverbend Yoga Studio was created to provide a peaceful, supportive environment where people can improve their well-being through yoga.

Our mission is to make yoga welcoming and accessible to people with different backgrounds, abilities, schedules, and levels of experience.

Accessibility and Accommodations

We believe yoga should be available to everyone. Our instructors provide pose modifications and encourage students to practice within their own comfort levels.

  • Beginner-friendly instruction
  • Options for seated and supported poses
  • Chairs and yoga props when needed
  • Slower-paced class options
  • Encouragement to take breaks whenever necessary

Please contact us before class if you have an accommodation request so we can discuss how to support your visit.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive a few minutes before class begins.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that allows movement.
  • Bring a water bottle and yoga mat if you have one.
  • Respect the personal space of other students.
  • Inform your instructor if you need a modification.
  • Please contact the studio if you need to cancel.
