Monthly Workshops

At Riverbend Yoga Studio, we offer workshops that encourage relaxation, movement, mindfulness, and connection. All experience levels are welcome!

Beginner Yoga Workshop

Learn basic yoga poses, breathing techniques, and ways to build confidence in your practice.

Date: First Saturday of each month

Time: 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM

Mindfulness and Meditation

Practice relaxation, guided meditation, and mindful breathing to help manage everyday stress.

Date: Second Saturday of each month

Time: 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM

Restorative Yoga Workshop

Enjoy gentle stretching, supported poses, and peaceful relaxation in a welcoming environment.

Date: Third Saturday of each month

Time: 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM
