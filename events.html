Explore Our Monthly Workshops

At Riverbend Yoga Studio, we believe that wellness is a journey best shared with others. Our monthly workshops provide opportunities to learn new skills, practice mindfulness, and connect with our welcoming community.

Whether you are new to yoga or have years of experience, our workshops offer a supportive and relaxing environment. Modifications are available for different abilities and comfort levels.

Monthly Workshop Offerings

Explore our sample monthly offerings below. Contact the studio for upcoming dates, availability, and registration information.

Beginner Yoga Foundations

Learn basic yoga poses, breathing techniques, and safe movement practices. This workshop is ideal for beginners who want to feel confident starting their yoga journey.

Mindful Breathing and Relaxation

Discover simple breathing exercises and relaxation techniques designed to reduce everyday stress and encourage mindfulness. All experience levels are welcome.

Gentle Stretching and Restorative Yoga

Enjoy a slower-paced workshop focused on flexibility, supported poses, and relaxation. This workshop is especially welcoming to older adults and anyone seeking gentle movement.

Featured Workshop

Relax, Restore, and Recharge

Take time for yourself with guided breathing, gentle stretching, and restorative yoga. This welcoming workshop is designed to help participants unwind and restore balance.
