Explore Our Yoga Classes

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers different styles of yoga to support a variety of goals, abilities, and experience levels. Our instructors encourage everyone to listen to their bodies and move comfortably.

Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga focuses on slow movements, stretching, breathing, and balance. This class is ideal for beginners, older adults, and anyone who prefers a relaxed practice.

Vinyasa Yoga

Vinyasa Yoga connects movement with breathing. Students move through a series of flowing poses. Different modifications are offered to accommodate individual comfort and experience levels.

Restorative Yoga

Restorative Yoga uses comfortable positions and relaxation techniques to encourage rest and stress relief. It is a slower-paced class designed to help students relax and recharge.

Private Yoga Sessions

Private sessions provide individual attention from an instructor. Students can discuss their goals and receive guidance tailored to their comfort level, interests, and schedule.
