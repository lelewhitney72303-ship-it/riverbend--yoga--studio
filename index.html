Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Riverbend Yoga Studio is a welcoming space where people of all ages and experience levels can explore movement, relaxation, and mindfulness.

Our mission is to support physical wellness and emotional balance through accessible yoga classes and a friendly community.

New to yoga? You are welcome here! You do not need to be flexible or have previous experience. Our instructors offer guidance and modifications so you can participate comfortably at your own pace.

Featured Class: Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga is a beginner-friendly class focused on comfortable stretching, breathing, balance, and relaxation. It is a wonderful choice for older adults and anyone looking for a slower-paced practice.
